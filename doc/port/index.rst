=========================================
ShootArchitecture, the metafspm port
=========================================

ShootArchitecture (``openalea.adel.architecture``) is the port of Adel's dynamic shoot architecture to a metafspm
``StructuralComponent``: the plants' topology in metafspm's MPG, the organs' dimensions and leaf shapes from Adel's
tables, the elements from the organs' lengths, their placement by Adel's turtle and their triangles, for light models
and display. ``ScenarioDevelopment`` (``openalea.adel.development``) gives the organs' lengths from a simple
scenario. The developer tutorials are in ``example/tutorials/developer/``, the users' in ``example/tutorials/user/``;
the examples are in ``example/``.
